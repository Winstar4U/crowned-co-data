Research conducted by direct, read-only browsing of each supplier's own website plus third-party review/forum sources. No accounts created, no forms submitted, no orders placed. Each claim is labeled:
- **VERIFIED** — read directly on the supplier's own site
- **REPORTED** — third-party source (review site, blog, forum)
- **UNKNOWN** — not found after a genuine search attempt (noted what was tried)
