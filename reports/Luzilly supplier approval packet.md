## G. Executive summary

Twenty bridal/wedding suppliers and channels were researched (the 9 originally named candidates,
2 confirmed Shopify Collective-connected brands, and 9 additional worldwide leads). **None clears a
GREEN rating.** Three are workable-but-imperfect (YELLOW: **AW Bridal, UCALLA, BrydealoFactory**) —
their dropship mechanics work, but wholesale-specific return terms are either unverified or
documented-but-restrictive (store credit only, 35% restocking fee). Nine sit at UNKNOWN because
pricing or return terms are gated behind a login or private connection never accessed in this
read-only pass — including both confirmed Shopify Collective bridal participants, **Dare and Dazzle**
and **Lara New York**. Eight are RED, most notably **KissBridal** and **SMC Fashion** — ironically the
two best-documented, most professionally run candidates — because each one's own wholesale policy
states final sale/no returns, directly conflicting with Luzilly's buyer's-remorse requirement.
**Everly Bridals has permanently closed** and should be dropped outright.

**Near-term profitable opportunity, prioritized:** none of the three YELLOW suppliers can be
greenlit today. The highest-value near-term action is not picking a supplier — it's sending the
same short written question set (restocking fee, who pays return shipping, refund-vs-credit, and
whether a wholesale/dropship policy differs from the consumer policy at all) to **AW Bridal, UCALLA,
and BrydealoFactory in parallel**, and opening a connection request to **Dare and Dazzle** on Shopify
Collective (the only option that structurally guarantees the supplier, not Luzilly, handles the
physical return). All three YELLOW suppliers' pricing clears a 3.0x-markup net margin comfortably
above 20% even at a 30% promotional discount and a stress-tested 25% return rate — the blocking issue
is documented return-cost recovery, not the headline markup.

## C. Supplier-return liability & worst-case cash-exposure assessment

- **BrydealoFactory:** a returned unit is a quantified, bounded loss — roughly **$371 cash-negative**
  per returned unit at the modeled mid-price point (sunk landed cost + sunk payment fee + return
  freight), offset only by non-cash store credit (65% of landed cost, usable only on a future
  Brydealo order). At a 25% return rate, blended margin still clears 20%, but every return is a
  documented cash loss with no recovery path.
- **UCALLA:** bounded between "minor exposure" (best case: no restocking fee, supplier eats return
  shipping) and "exposure comparable to BrydealoFactory or worse" (conservative case: Luzilly pays
  international return freight plus an unknown restocking fee). Genuinely unresolved pending written
  confirmation — not a documented bad term, but not a confirmed good one either.
- **1stopbridal:** **unbounded exposure** — no return/refund/defect policy exists in writing at all,
  so a return could mean total loss of the landed cost with zero recovery. This independently
  confirms its RED rating regardless of otherwise-healthy pricing.
- **Across every RED-rated supplier with documented final-sale wholesale terms** (KissBridal, SMC
  Fashion, Farinosa Bridal, Wedding Factory Direct): the worst case is not ambiguous — it is the
  supplier's own stated policy. Any order from these suppliers must be treated by Luzilly as
  effectively non-returnable to the supplier, meaning Luzilly would self-fund 100% of any customer
  refund with zero wholesale-side recovery, which is precisely the inventory/liability exposure the
  operating model forbids.
- **Working-capital implication:** until a supplier's written return terms (not consumer terms) are
  in hand, Luzilly should assume **worst case = full landed cost unrecoverable per return** for
  budgeting purposes, and size any pilot batch accordingly (small, single-SKU test orders only, not
  a catalog-wide launch) once a YELLOW supplier is actually approved.

## D. Shopify Collective eligibility & connection findings

See **Section 3** of the main report for full detail. Summary:
- **Mechanism:** Collective's return flow is retailer-configurable, not platform-mandated; Luzilly
  (the retailer) always refunds the customer, and the supplier separately reimburses Luzilly per
  their private agreement. The only configuration compatible with Luzilly's no-warehouse model is
  "supplier creates the return label" + "automatically refund customer" — but Luzilly still carries
  first-line customer-service and chargeback exposure until the supplier's reimbursement posts.
- **CONFIRMED currently connected, bridal-relevant:** Dare and Dazzle (wedding gowns/bespoke bridal)
  and Lara New York (mother-of-the-bride/evening). Neither brand's actual Collective pricing or
  return-policy configuration was accessible without sending a connection request — both remain
  UNKNOWN as onboardable suppliers despite being confirmed as participants.
- **UNVERIFIED POSSIBLE (not upgraded to confirmed):** Iyza Belaar, A & M Enterprises Bridal.
- **NOT FOUND:** No bridal apparel brand appears in Shopify's own Collective marketing examples or
  case studies; bridal is not a curated vertical within Collective today.
